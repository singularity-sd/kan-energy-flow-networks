# KAN in an Energy Flow Network
Protocol fixed before the new training. The historical pilot on QG_jets and QG_jets_1 is exploratory; the old folder also contains oss_19.parquet, so shard 19 is not considered fully blind. The primary test is shard 18, never used by this study to choose models.
- Pythia light-quark vs gluon: training shards 0 and 2; validation shard 1; test shards 18 (primary) and 19 (replication). Herwig light-quark shard 1, evaluated without retraining. 100,000 jets per shard.
- Same coordinates, normalised pT weights, 16 observables. Baseline EFN with SiLU MLPs; KAN variant with univariate cubic splines in the encoder and in the head. Comparable capacity; secondary variant with a KAN encoder and an MLP head to separate the two contributions.
- Three seeds (17,29,43), 6 epochs, AdamW, batch 512, lr 0.003 with cosine decay. Checkpoints chosen only by validation AUC. Metrics per seed and for the ensemble of logits. FPR at 50% and 80% quark efficiency as descriptive. Paired per-jet bootstrap on the ensemble delta AUC (300 replicates); it does not include uncertainty between generators or between trainings.
- Predefined net improvement: delta AUC >= 0.003 on the primary, bootstrap lower bound > 0, mean per-seed delta positive also on shard 19 and Herwig. Secondary analyses do not replace the primary. The threshold and the budget describe this study; they are not a standard threshold of the field.
- Numerical checks on the model: permutation, 40/60 collinear splitting and addition of a constituent with a pT fraction of 1e-7. Input without PID and without truncation of the constituents.
- Budget sensitivity analysis, added during training and before the primary test after seeing the first validation curves: 16 epochs for both networks (seed 17), Pythia 18 and Herwig 1. Interpreted separately.
